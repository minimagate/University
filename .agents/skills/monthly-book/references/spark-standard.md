# Spark teaching standard

The user found the older books too difficult and not step by step enough. Spark books should be simple, clear and mentally light, while keeping every main concept and full mathematical rigor. Assume school arithmetic, fractions and elementary algebra, not university proof experience; build any other prerequisite where it is needed.

## Tone

Write like a good teacher explaining to one student: simple, warm, patient and precise, in natural Italian that is pleasant to read. Explain why things are done, not only what is done, and do not hide a real step behind «ovviamente» or «è immediato». No infantilizing language, forced jokes or exclamation marks, and no remarks addressed to a reviewer rather than the reader.

How to get there (structure, sentence rhythm, how to open a chapter, how to lead into a definition or proof) is left to the writer's judgment. Write the book well rather than to a checklist.

## Content

- Cover all main concepts of the course. Make them easy to find in titles and the contents.
- Keep proofs complete and correct, with the hypotheses that matter.
- Provide exercises with explained solutions.
- Keep the reading load manageable by selecting secondary material and trimming duplication rather than concepts, and record omissions in `coverage.md`.

## Editorial identity

Classical university book: Computer Modern, well-composed formulas, sober hierarchy, monochrome fine-line vector illustrations, discreet boxes and balanced spacing. Retain useful existing assets. Sources, coverage and corrections live in companion files. Spark is the default for all courses; adapt to the discipline.
